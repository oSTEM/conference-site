## Frequently Asked Questions

#### I'm getting an error that says my member ID is valid but not eligible for a specific ticket.

This applies to registrations where you get errors that look like this:

<blockquote className="member-id-quote">
  <p><em>The Member ID “(your ID)” is not eligible for ”Collegiate / Professional (Member) - Full Conference”. This ticket requires membership level(s): collegiate chapter membership, collegiate global membership, etc...</em></p>
</blockquote>

If your account does not currently show an active oSTEM membership, please update it in YourMembership. Visit the [Membership page](https://ostem.org/members/membership.asp) (can also be reached under Payments & History &rarr; Membership), select “Upgrade your membership”, choose the membership option that matches your status (collegiate or professional, with or without a chapter), and submit the form. Once complete, your member ID should work for registration; please allow up to 24 hours for Whova to sync the updated membership. There are no additional dues for this change, and donations are optional.
